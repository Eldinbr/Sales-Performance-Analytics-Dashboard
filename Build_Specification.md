# Power BI Build Specification

## Page 1 — Executive Overview
KPI cards: Total Sales, Total Profit, Profit Margin, Total Orders, Average Order Value.
Charts: Sales & Profit Trend, Sales by Region, Profit by Category, Sales by Segment, Top 10 Products.

## Page 2 — Product Analysis
Cards: Categories, Products, Units Sold, Average Discount.
Charts: Sales by Category, Sales vs Profit by Product, Top 10 Products, Bottom 10 Products.

## Page 3 — Regional & Customer Analysis
Charts: Sales by Region, Profit by Region, Sales by Customer Segment, Top Customers.

## Slicers
Year, Quarter, Month, Region, Category, Sub-Category, Segment.

## Formatting
16:9 pages, clean light canvas, consistent GBP currency, clear titles, enabled cross-filtering, useful report-page tooltips.
